Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

This policy supplements the WalkWorthy Privacy Policy and describes how WalkWorthy handles consumer health data. WalkWorthy provides general spiritual encouragement and personal reflection; it is not a medical, mental-health, diagnostic, treatment, or crisis service.

Consumer health data we collect

When you choose to submit a check-in, we collect:

  • Your mood score and mood label.
  • Emotion tags and selected life-impact areas, which can include faith.
  • Your follow-up score and any optional note you write with the check-in.
  • The date and check-in period (morning, midday, or evening).

We derive mood summaries, Scripture-based encouragements, and daily reflections from that information. Journal entries are stored on your device and are not collected by us in the current iOS app. Optional Firebase Analytics events do not contain check-in, journal, or profile content.

Sources

We receive consumer health data directly from the choices and optional text you submit in WalkWorthy. We create the derived summaries and generated responses described above from those submissions. We do not buy consumer health data from data brokers or obtain it from social networks, advertisers, public records, or health-care providers.

Why we collect and use it

  • To save and show the private check-in and mood history you request.
  • To generate the encouragement and daily reflection you request.
  • To screen optional notes and generated prose for safety.
  • To secure the service, prevent abuse, investigate errors, and comply with law.

We will not collect or use additional categories of consumer health data, or use this data for a new purpose, without first updating this policy and obtaining any consent required by law.

Who receives it

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud processes check-ins, derived mood summaries, generated responses, account identifiers, and related request/security data to host and secure your private account data.
  • OpenAI processes mood scores and labels, emotion tags, life areas, and an optional note for safety screening and generation only after you turn on “Share check-ins with OpenAI.” If profile personalization is also on, it may receive an adult age range, occupation or major, and hobbies. It does not receive your name, email, Firebase user ID, gender, or on-device journal.

These providers act as processors for the purposes disclosed above. We have no affiliates with whom we share consumer health data. We do not sell consumer health data or disclose it to data brokers, advertisers, social networks, or other third parties for their independent use.

Your consumer health data rights

  • Access and confirmation — view your check-ins and generated responses in the app, or email us to request confirmation, a copy, or a list of recipients.
  • Withdraw permission — turn off “Share check-ins with OpenAI” to stop future OpenAI processing. Turn off profile personalization separately to stop future use of profile details. You can stop future check-in collection by not submitting another check-in.
  • Delete — Settings → Account → Delete Account removes your account, cloud check-ins, summaries, generated responses, and local data. You may also email us with a deletion request.
  • Appeal — if we deny a request, reply to our decision with “Appeal” in the subject line and explain why you believe the decision should be reconsidered.

Submit a request to walkworthyofficial@gmail.com. We may ask for information reasonably needed to authenticate the request. We will respond without undue delay and within 45 days; if reasonably necessary, we may extend once by another 45 days and will explain the extension during the initial period. Requests are free up to twice per year unless applicable law permits a charge for manifestly unfounded, excessive, or repetitive requests.

Retention and security

Cloud check-ins, summaries, profile data, and generated responses are kept until you delete your account. Provider backup copies may take time to age out after deletion, but we handle deletion requests within the deadlines required by applicable law. We use access controls, authenticated requests, App Check, HTTPS, owner-scoped database rules, rate limits, data minimization, and protected local storage as described in the main Privacy Policy. No system can guarantee perfect security.

Contact

Questions or requests can be sent to walkworthyofficial@gmail.com.
