WalkWorthy

AI Safety Information

Updated: September 4, 2026

What the AI feature is

WalkWorthy uses OpenAI to write Scripture-based encouragement and daily reflections. The words around a passage are generated by AI and can be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable. Scripture quotations come from a fixed server catalog rather than from the AI model. AI output is labeled in the app.

WalkWorthy is for general spiritual encouragement. It is not a person, therapist, health-care provider, diagnostic tool, treatment, crisis service, or substitute for professional care. The app does not monitor you or contact emergency services, family, clergy, or clinicians on your behalf.

Your choice and your data

Before WalkWorthy sends check-in information to OpenAI, the app names OpenAI, lists the information involved, and asks you to turn AI sharing on. You may decline and continue using features that do not require AI. You can withdraw permission in Settings. A withdrawal takes effect on the device immediately; the app reports if it still needs a connection to confirm the change with the server.

Avoid putting names, contact details, medical records, passwords, or information about another person in a note. See the Privacy Policy and Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for the data sent, retention periods, service providers, and privacy rights.

Safety handling

  • Optional notes are screened before generation.
  • Generated prose is screened before it is displayed or stored.
  • If screening is unavailable, the app uses fixed, human-written content instead of unscreened generated prose.
  • If screening identifies a self-harm signal in a submitted note, the app shows fixed support information rather than an AI-generated response.

Automated screening can miss, misunderstand, or incorrectly block content. Do not rely on WalkWorthy to recognize an emergency.

Get help now

If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In the United States and its territories, call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Outside the United States, use your local emergency or crisis service.

Report a concern

Use the report link beside a generated response or email walkworthyofficial@gmail.com. The app opens an email draft and does not attach your check-in, note, account ID, or generated response automatically. Include only the information you choose to share.
