WalkWorthy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 6, 2026

WalkWorthy is a Scripture-based encouragement app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights. The app is offered by Nathan Jones ("we", "us").

We take privacy seriously. Mood and journal content is among the most sensitive data we handle, and the sections below describe exactly what happens to it.

Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to the WalkWorthy iOS app and the supporting cloud services we operate. It does not apply to other apps or websites that we do not control, even if those services are linked from within WalkWorthy.

Information we collect

We collect only the information we need to make the app work and to personalize it.

Information you provide

  • Email address — required to create an account.
  • First name — shown on the home screen greeting.
  • Optional profile details — age, occupation, field of study, gender, and hobbies. Used to personalize the tone of encouragements.
  • Mood check-ins — the numeric score you select, emotion tags you pick, impact categories, and any free-text note you add.
  • Journal entries — the free-text content you write. Journal entries are stored on your device only and are not synced to our servers by default.
  • Notification preferences — your chosen check-in reminder times.

Information we generate or receive automatically

  • Firebase user identifier (UID) — an opaque internal identifier assigned when you sign up. Used to link your data to your account.
  • Device identifiers — an App Attest token issued by Apple, used to confirm that requests come from a genuine, un-tampered copy of the app. We do not receive the IDFA.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — when the app crashes we collect technical details (stack trace, OS version, device model) via Firebase Crashlytics. We do not collect the content of your mood entries or journal in these reports.
  • Request logs — timestamps, IP address, and endpoint called, stored by our backend for a short period to support rate limiting and abuse prevention.

How we use your information

  • To operate the core features of the app (sign-in, mood check-ins, daily reflections, journal).
  • To personalize the Scripture-based encouragements we generate for you, based on your profile and mood signals.
  • To send notifications at the times you have configured.
  • To prevent abuse, enforce rate limits, and protect the integrity of the service.
  • To diagnose and fix bugs and crashes.

We do not use your information to sell advertising, track you across other apps or websites, or build a profile for marketing purposes. We do not have advertising partners.

Who we share information with

We share your information only with the service providers we need to run the app. These providers act as "sub-processors" — they process data on our behalf under contract.

  • Google Firebase (Google Cloud) — hosts our database, authenticates your account, stores mood and profile data, and verifies device integrity. Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device and never touch our servers. Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure in the United States. See Firebase's Privacy and Security page.
  • OpenAI — when you submit a mood check-in or request a daily reflection, we send the structured signals (mood score, emotion tags, profile context) to OpenAI so it can generate a personalized Scripture-based response. The app asks for your explicit consent before your first check-in, and nothing is sent to OpenAI until you agree. We have configured our OpenAI project for Zero Data Retention, which means OpenAI does not retain this data after generating the response. We do not send your email address, first name, Firebase UID, or journal entries to OpenAI.
  • Firebase Analytics — with your permission, we count anonymous feature usage (for example, "a check-in was completed") to understand which parts of the app matter. Analytics events never include your check-in content, notes, or profile details, and you can turn this off anytime in Settings → AI & Your Data.
  • Apple — if you sign in with your Apple ID, Apple handles the authentication and returns a signed identity token. See Apple's privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We do not disclose your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.

How long we keep your information

  • Mood check-ins and profile data — until you delete your account.
  • Journal entries — stored only on your device. If you reinstall or switch devices, journal entries are not recoverable.
  • Daily reflections — generated on demand and cached for the current day only.
  • Request logs and rate-limit counters — automatically expired within 30 days.
  • Crash reports — retained by Firebase Crashlytics per Google's default retention (90 days).

When you delete your account via Settings → Account → Delete Account, we permanently remove your profile, mood history, daily reflections, encouragements, device tokens, and authentication record. This is immediate and irreversible.

Your rights and choices

  • Access — you can see all of your data directly in the app (mood history, journal entries, profile). If you need an export in another format, email us and we will provide it within 30 days.
  • Correction — you can update your profile in Settings at any time.
  • Deletion — use Settings → Account → Delete Account to remove everything. If you prefer we handle it manually, email us.
  • Disable notifications — toggle reminders off in Settings.
  • Opt out of personalization — the profile-based personalization toggle in Settings disables the use of your profile details to shape encouragements. Core mood signals are still used.

If you are a resident of California, the European Union, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with specific data protection rights, you have the rights described above plus the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Contact us first and we will do our best to resolve any concern directly.

Children's privacy

WalkWorthy is intended for users age 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The sign-up flow asks for age and rejects anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please email us and we will delete it promptly.

Parents and guardians of users aged 13-17: we encourage you to review this policy with your child and supervise their use of the app.

Security

We protect your data with multiple layers of security:

  • All traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Firebase Authentication issues short-lived ID tokens that are verified on every request.
  • Firebase App Check + Apple App Attest confirm requests come from a genuine copy of the app, not a tampered build or script.
  • On-device data is stored with iOS Data Protection (Complete) so it is unreadable while the device is locked.
  • Device push tokens are hashed with SHA-256 plus a server-side secret before storage so we never retain a plain push token.
  • Firestore security rules enforce per-user access — no user can read or write another user's data, even at the database level.

No system is perfectly secure. If you become aware of a security issue, please email us at the address below.

International users

WalkWorthy is operated from the United States. If you use the app from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S. By using the app, you consent to this transfer.

If we make material changes, we will notify you in-app or by email before the changes take effect. Continuing to use the app after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.
