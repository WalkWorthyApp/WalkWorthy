WalkWorthy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

WalkWorthy is a Scripture-based encouragement app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights. The app is offered by Nathan Jones ("we", "us").

We take privacy seriously. Mood information and religious beliefs can be sensitive, and the sections below describe what happens to it. Journal entries remain on your device in the current iOS app and are not collected by us.

See our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for additional disclosures and rights concerning mood and emotional-wellness data.

Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to the WalkWorthy iOS app and the supporting cloud services we operate. It does not apply to other apps or websites that we do not control, even if those services are linked from within WalkWorthy.

Information we collect

We collect information needed to operate the app and information you optionally provide.

Information you provide

  • Account information — your email address for email/password accounts, or the identifiers and any email relay address supplied through Sign in with Apple.
  • Display name — optional and shown only in the home-screen greeting.
  • Age eligibility and optional profile details — the age you enter is used on-device to enforce the 18+ minimum. When you grant AI permission, we store your self-declared confirmation that you are 18 or older with your permission record. This is a declaration, not identity verification. We may also store an optional profile age range. You may also provide an occupation, field of study, and hobbies. Earlier test versions also collected gender; that field is no longer collected or sent to our AI provider and is removed when the profile is next updated or the account is deleted.
  • Mood check-ins — the numeric score you select, emotion tags you pick, impact categories (which can include faith), and any free-text note you add.
  • Journal entries — the free-text content you write. Journal entries are stored on your device only and are not sent to our servers or AI provider by the current iOS app. Earlier versions or clients may have stored cloud journal entries; existing cloud entries can still be accessed or deleted, but new cloud journal writes are no longer accepted.
  • Notification preferences — your chosen check-in reminder times, stored on your device. Reminders are local notifications; we do not collect a push notification token.

Free-text notes, hobbies, and other optional fields may reveal health information, religious beliefs, or identifying details about you or others. Avoid entering names, contact information, medical records, or other details unnecessary for your reflection.

Information we generate or receive automatically

  • Derived content — mood summaries, inferred overall sentiment, generated encouragements and daily reflections linked to your account.
  • AI permission record — your sharing choice, disclosure version, self-declared age group, revision and server-recorded update time. We use this to enforce your choice across devices and prevent outdated requests from changing it.
  • Firebase user identifier (UID) — an opaque internal identifier assigned when you sign up. Used to link your data to your account.
  • App and device identifiers — Firebase assigns installation or app-instance identifiers, and Apple App Attest supplies integrity assertions used to help verify that requests come from a genuine copy of the app. We do not use these identifiers to track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Crash, performance, and diagnostic data — Firebase Crashlytics may receive crash stack traces, app state, app version, OS version, device model, and related technical identifiers. We do not intentionally add your mood entries, notes, or journal content to crash reports.
  • Network and request data — our cloud providers receive IP addresses, timestamps, endpoint and request metadata, and security signals needed to deliver the service, diagnose failures, and prevent abuse. Our application no longer creates IP-address-based Firestore rate-limit records; older such records and provider network logs may remain subject to retention and deletion processes.
  • Optional analytics — only if you turn analytics on, Firebase Analytics receives app interactions (such as a feature being used), an app-instance identifier, device/app information, and coarse location derived from a masked IP address. Analytics never receives mood check-ins, notes, journal text, or profile fields from our event code.

How we use your information

  • To operate the core features of the app (sign-in, mood check-ins, daily reflections, journal).
  • To personalize the Scripture-based encouragements we generate for you, based on your profile and mood signals.
  • To send notifications at the times you have configured.
  • To prevent abuse, enforce rate limits, and protect the integrity of the service.
  • To diagnose and fix bugs and crashes.

We do not use your information to sell advertising, track you across other apps or websites, or build a profile for marketing purposes. We do not have advertising partners.

Who we share information with

We share your information only with the service providers we need to run the app. These providers process data on our behalf. We require them to handle personal information consistently with this policy and to provide the same or equivalent protection.

  • Google Firebase (Google Cloud) — hosts our database, authenticates your account, stores mood, generated encouragement, reflection, and profile data, provides crash reporting and optional analytics, and verifies device integrity. Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device and never touch our servers. Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure in the United States. See Firebase's Privacy and Security page.
  • OpenAI — when you submit a mood check-in, we send the mood score and level, follow-up rating, emotion tags, selected life areas, check-in period, and an optional note to OpenAI so it can generate a response. For a daily reflection, we send a seven-day summary containing check-in dates, morning/midday/evening mood levels, and overall sentiment. If you separately enable profile personalization, we may also send an adult age range, occupation or field of study, and hobbies. The app asks for explicit permission before the first AI request, including safety screening, and you can withdraw that permission in Settings. We do not deliberately include your account email address, display name, Firebase UID, gender, or on-device journal entries in OpenAI requests. Optional text can still contain identifying or sensitive information that you enter. API response storage and Agents SDK tracing are disabled for these requests. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models unless a customer opts in. We do not opt in. Disabling response storage and tracing does not mean zero retention: OpenAI documents standard generation abuse-monitoring retention of up to 30 days, subject to legal exceptions and any approved account controls; moderation has a separate retention policy. See OpenAI API data controls. We also use OpenAI's moderation service to screen an optional note before generation and generated prose before it is shown. If a note is classified as a self-harm signal, we skip AI generation entirely and return a fixed, human-written response that offers crisis-support contact information. The app only displays that information — it never contacts anyone on your behalf. If safety screening is unavailable, the app uses fixed, human-written content instead of delivering unscreened generated prose. Automated screening can miss concerning language; it is not a crisis assessment or a monitored service. Our AI safety information explains the limitations and ways to find support or report a concern.
  • Firebase Analytics — only if you opt in, we count app interactions (for example, that a check-in was completed) to understand which features are used. We disable advertising-ID capability, IDFV collection, and ad-personalization signals. Analytics events never include check-in content, notes, journals, or profile details, and you can turn analytics off anytime in Settings.
  • Apple — if you sign in with your Apple ID, Apple handles the authentication and returns a signed identity token. See Apple's privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We do not disclose your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.

How long we keep your information

  • Mood check-ins, generated encouragements, profile data, daily reflections, AI permission records, and any legacy cloud journals — retained to provide your account history until you delete your account or make an applicable deletion request.
  • Journal entries — stored only on your device and removed when you sign out or delete your account. If you reinstall or switch devices, journal entries are not recoverable.
  • Local server-response snapshots — cached on your device for faster loading and removed when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Account rate-limit and daily AI-budget records — used for short abuse-prevention windows and marked for automatic expiration. A timestamp does not mean immediate deletion; provider expiration processing can take additional time. Legacy IP-based records are not reliably attributable to one account and follow the applicable security-retention and deletion process. Cloud request logs follow provider retention settings.
  • Deletion-security record — a minimal account identifier and deletion state prevent in-flight requests from recreating your data. Completed records are marked to expire after seven days. Unfinished records remain until cleanup is resolved, and we automatically retry failed cleanup. You can also retry deletion in the app or contact support. These records contain no mood notes or generated content.
  • Crash and analytics records — retained according to our configured Firebase/Google retention settings or deleted when no longer needed for the stated purpose.

When you delete your account via Settings → Account → Delete Account, we permanently remove your profile, mood history, daily reflections, encouragements, authentication record, permission record, any legacy cloud journals, local journal, local response snapshots, and local notification preferences. Device data removal starts as soon as you confirm deletion, even if the server request fails. The app retains a minimal local account identifier and cleanup status to finish device cleanup after a restart and prevent data from being loaded again while deletion is unresolved. Once the server confirms acceptance, failed server cleanup is retried automatically. If acceptance cannot be confirmed, retry the request or contact support. The app does not store push tokens. Account deletion cannot be undone. It does not mean every separate security log or provider backup disappears instantly. We handle remaining copies and recipient deletion under applicable law, as described in our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Your rights and choices

  • Access — you can see your main account content directly in the app (mood history, journal entries, profile). If you need an export in another format, email us for an authenticated access or portability request. We respond within the applicable deadlines described below.
  • Correction — you can update your profile in Settings at any time.
  • Deletion — use Settings → Account → Delete Account to remove everything. If you prefer we handle it manually, email us.
  • Disable notifications — toggle reminders off in Settings.
  • Opt out of personalization — the profile-based personalization toggle in Settings disables the use of your profile details to shape encouragements. Core mood signals are still used.
  • Withdraw AI permission — turn off “Share check-ins with OpenAI” in Settings. After the withdrawal is saved to your account, the server blocks new AI requests across devices. If saving fails, the app stops AI locally and offers a retry; other devices are not updated until the server receives the change. A request already sent to OpenAI cannot reliably be recalled. Existing history, on-device journaling and account deletion remain available. The current combined check-in/generation flow requires AI permission to submit new check-ins.
  • Disable analytics — analytics is off by default and can be switched on or off independently in Settings.

Depending on applicable law, you may also request restriction, object to processing, or appeal a refused request. We do not discriminate for exercising privacy rights. Contact walkworthyofficial@gmail.com; we may request proportionate identity verification. General requests are handled without undue delay and within the applicable legal period: one calendar month under GDPR/UK GDPR where applicable, or generally 45 days under applicable US privacy laws. Any permitted extension will be explained within the initial period. For consumer health deletion and appeals, see the specific deadlines in our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. You may complain directly to the relevant regulator without contacting us first.

We do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not track your activity across other companies' services. The app does not interpret browser Do Not Track signals; our no-tracking practice applies regardless. We do not enable third parties to collect data through the app for tracking across unrelated services. Optional service analytics remains subject to your separate choice.

Children's privacy

WalkWorthy is an adults-only service, intended for users age 18 and older. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. Before account creation, the app requires the person signing up to confirm that they are at least 18; onboarding also rejects an age below 18, and AI permission requires a separate adult confirmation. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, please email us and we will delete it promptly.

Our age confirmation is self-declared: it is a statement by the user, not verified identity or age assurance. We do not use it for advertising. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor is using WalkWorthy, contact us and we will remove the account.

Security

We protect your data with multiple layers of security:

  • All traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Firebase Authentication issues short-lived ID tokens that are verified on every request.
  • Firebase App Check + Apple App Attest confirm requests come from a genuine copy of the app, not a tampered build or script.
  • Local journal data and authenticated server-response snapshots are stored with iOS complete file protection and excluded from device backups.
  • Firestore security rules enforce per-user access — no user can read or write another user's data, even at the database level.

No system is perfectly secure. If you become aware of a security issue, please email us at the address below.

International users

WalkWorthy is operated from the United States. If you use the app from outside the U.S., your information may be transferred to and processed in the U.S. Those locations may have different data-protection laws than your country. Contact us if you have a question about the safeguards used for an international transfer.
