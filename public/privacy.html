WalkWorthy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

WalkWorthy is a Scripture-based encouragement app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights. The app is offered by Nathan Jones ("we", "us").

We take privacy seriously. Mood information can be sensitive, and the sections below describe what happens to it. Journal entries remain on your device in the current iOS app and are not collected by us.

See our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for additional disclosures and rights concerning mood and emotional-wellness data.

Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to the WalkWorthy iOS app and the supporting cloud services we operate. It does not apply to other apps or websites that we do not control, even if those services are linked from within WalkWorthy.

Information we collect

We collect information needed to operate the app and information you optionally provide.

Information you provide

  • Account information — your email address for email/password accounts, or the identifiers and any email relay address supplied through Sign in with Apple.
  • Display name — optional and shown only in the home-screen greeting.
  • Age eligibility and optional profile details — the age you enter is used on-device to enforce the 13+ minimum. For adults, we may store an age range. You may also provide an occupation, field of study, and hobbies. Earlier test versions also collected gender; that field is no longer collected or sent to our AI provider and is removed when the profile is next updated or the account is deleted.
  • Mood check-ins — the numeric score you select, emotion tags you pick, impact categories (which can include faith), and any free-text note you add.
  • Journal entries — the free-text content you write. Journal entries are stored on your device only and are not sent to our servers or AI provider by the current iOS app.
  • Notification preferences — your chosen check-in reminder times, stored on your device. Reminders are local notifications; we do not collect a push notification token.

Information we generate or receive automatically

  • Firebase user identifier (UID) — an opaque internal identifier assigned when you sign up. Used to link your data to your account.
  • App and device identifiers — Firebase assigns installation or app-instance identifiers, and Apple App Attest supplies integrity assertions used to help verify that requests come from a genuine copy of the app. We do not use these identifiers to track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Crash, performance, and diagnostic data — Firebase Crashlytics may receive crash stack traces, app state, app version, OS version, device model, and related technical identifiers. We do not intentionally add your mood entries, notes, or journal content to crash reports.
  • Network and request data — our cloud providers receive IP addresses, timestamps, endpoint and request metadata, and security signals needed to deliver the service, diagnose failures, prevent abuse, and enforce rate limits.
  • Optional analytics — only if you turn analytics on, Firebase Analytics receives app interactions (such as a feature being used), an app-instance identifier, device/app information, and coarse location derived from a masked IP address. Analytics never receives mood check-ins, notes, journal text, or profile fields from our event code.

How we use your information

  • To operate the core features of the app (sign-in, mood check-ins, daily reflections, journal).
  • To personalize the Scripture-based encouragements we generate for you, based on your profile and mood signals.
  • To send notifications at the times you have configured.
  • To prevent abuse, enforce rate limits, and protect the integrity of the service.
  • To diagnose and fix bugs and crashes.

We do not use your information to sell advertising, track you across other apps or websites, or build a profile for marketing purposes. We do not have advertising partners.

Who we share information with

We share your information only with the service providers we need to run the app. These providers process data on our behalf. We require them to handle personal information consistently with this policy and to provide the same or equivalent protection.

  • Google Firebase (Google Cloud) — hosts our database, authenticates your account, stores mood, generated encouragement, reflection, and profile data, provides crash reporting and optional analytics, and verifies device integrity. Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device and never touch our servers. Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure in the United States. See Firebase's Privacy and Security page.
  • OpenAI — when you submit a mood check-in, we send the mood score and level, follow-up rating, emotion tags, selected life areas, check-in period, and an optional note to OpenAI so it can generate a response. For a daily reflection, we send a seven-day summary containing check-in dates, morning/midday/evening mood levels, and overall sentiment. If you separately enable profile personalization, we may also send an adult age range, occupation or field of study, and hobbies. The app asks for explicit permission before the first AI request, and you can withdraw that permission in Settings. We do not send your email address, display name, Firebase UID, gender, or on-device journal entries to OpenAI. API response storage and Agents SDK tracing are disabled for these requests. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models unless a customer opts in. We do not opt in. We also use OpenAI's moderation service to screen an optional note before generation and generated prose before it is shown. If a note is classified as a self-harm signal, we skip AI generation entirely and return a fixed, human-written response that offers crisis-support contact information. The app only displays that information — it never contacts anyone on your behalf. If the moderation service is unavailable, the check-in still proceeds.
  • Firebase Analytics — only if you opt in, we count app interactions (for example, that a check-in was completed) to understand which features are used. We disable advertising-ID capability, IDFV collection, and ad-personalization signals. Analytics events never include check-in content, notes, journals, or profile details, and you can turn analytics off anytime in Settings.
  • Apple — if you sign in with your Apple ID, Apple handles the authentication and returns a signed identity token. See Apple's privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We do not disclose your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.

How long we keep your information

  • Mood check-ins, generated encouragements, profile data, and daily reflections — until you delete your account.
  • Journal entries — stored only on your device and removed when you sign out or delete your account. If you reinstall or switch devices, journal entries are not recoverable.
  • Local server-response snapshots — cached on your device for faster loading and removed when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Rate-limit records — used only for short hourly/daily abuse-prevention windows and marked for automatic expiration. Cloud request logs follow our provider-console retention settings.
  • Crash and analytics records — retained according to our configured Firebase/Google retention settings or deleted when no longer needed for the stated purpose.

When you delete your account via Settings → Account → Delete Account, we permanently remove your profile, mood history, daily reflections, encouragements, authentication record, local journal, local response snapshots, and local notification preferences. The app does not store push tokens. Deletion is irreversible; provider backup and log copies may age out under the providers' normal deletion schedules and applicable legal deadlines.

Your rights and choices

  • Access — you can see all of your data directly in the app (mood history, journal entries, profile). If you need an export in another format, email us and we will provide it within 30 days.
  • Correction — you can update your profile in Settings at any time.
  • Deletion — use Settings → Account → Delete Account to remove everything. If you prefer we handle it manually, email us.
  • Disable notifications — toggle reminders off in Settings.
  • Opt out of personalization — the profile-based personalization toggle in Settings disables the use of your profile details to shape encouragements. Core mood signals are still used.
  • Withdraw AI permission — turn off “Share check-ins with OpenAI” in Settings. New check-ins and daily-reflection requests will not be sent to OpenAI while it is off.
  • Disable analytics — analytics is off by default and can be switched on or off independently in Settings.

If you are a resident of California, the European Union, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with specific data protection rights, you have the rights described above plus the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Contact us first and we will do our best to resolve any concern directly.

Children's privacy

WalkWorthy is intended for users age 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Before account creation, the app requires the person signing up to confirm that they are at least 13; onboarding also rejects an age below 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please email us and we will delete it promptly.

Parents and guardians of users aged 13-17: we encourage you to review this policy with your child and supervise their use of the app.

Security

We protect your data with multiple layers of security:

  • All traffic between the app and our backend is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Firebase Authentication issues short-lived ID tokens that are verified on every request.
  • Firebase App Check + Apple App Attest confirm requests come from a genuine copy of the app, not a tampered build or script.
  • Local journal data and authenticated server-response snapshots are stored with iOS complete file protection and excluded from device backups.
  • Firestore security rules enforce per-user access — no user can read or write another user's data, even at the database level.

No system is perfectly secure. If you become aware of a security issue, please email us at the address below.

International users

WalkWorthy is operated from the United States. If you use the app from outside the U.S., your information may be transferred to and processed in the U.S. Those locations may have different data-protection laws than your country. Contact us if you have a question about the safeguards used for an international transfer.
